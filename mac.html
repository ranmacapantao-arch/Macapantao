Kurosaki Ichigo is the main character of the anime and manga series Bleach. He is a teenager who becomes

a Substitute Soul Reaper and gains the ability to fight evil spirits called Hollows. Ichigo is known for his

strong determination, courage, and desire to protect his family and friends. With his Zanpakutō, Zangetsu,

and his unique combination of Soul Reaper, Hollow, and Quincy powers, he becomes one of the strongest

warriors in the Bleach universe.
